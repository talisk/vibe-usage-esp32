# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a welcoming, technically rigorous place. Be
respectful, assume good intent while checking evidence, make room for different
experience levels, and critique ideas or code rather than people. Safety and
privacy concerns are valid contributions even when they slow a release.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized
language, threats, doxxing, publishing another person's credentials or private
device data, sustained disruption, and retaliation against a good-faith report.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, reviews, issue trackers,
community spaces, and public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
private reporting channel. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive
personal information. Maintainers will acknowledge a report, gather only the
information needed, protect confidentiality where possible, and choose a
proportionate response. Responses may include a private correction, warning,
temporary restriction, removal of content, or permanent exclusion.

Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are the subject of a report. A
good-faith report will not be punished, but knowingly false or abusive reports
may themselves violate this standard.
